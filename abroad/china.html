Engineering exchange in China

In the summer of 2025 I traveled across China with the SEEChina International Engineering Exchange as communications and data lead, meeting the people building China's AI and manufacturing.

When
May – Jul 2025
Where
China
Role

What I did

As communications and data lead, I led technical discussions with professors and directors during our visits. Being fluent in Mandarin meant I could keep conversations going directly, without waiting on translation.

Where we went

  1. Universities

    Tsinghua University, Peking University, Zhejiang University, and Fudan University. We talked with faculty about research directions in AI and engineering education.

  2. Technology companies

    Tencent, BYD, Xiaomi, DJI, and XAG. The focus was on how they move from research to products at scale, from consumer electronics and drones to electric vehicles and agricultural robotics.

Photos

What it taught me

Seeing drones, EVs, and farm robots built at that scale made it clear how tightly hardware, software, and manufacturing are linked. It's a big part of why I want to work where engineering meets real deployment.
